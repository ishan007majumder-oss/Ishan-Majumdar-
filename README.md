Introduction
Dyslexia is a common learning difficulty that mainly affects a person,s ability to read spell write and process written language.People with dyslexia may find it difficult to recognize words quickly,connect letters with their sounds,or understand written information easily.However, dyslexia is not related to a person,s intelligence. Many people with dyslexia have normal or high intelligence and can be talented in areas such as creativity, problem solving,arts and design,visual thinking etc.
Dyslexia common difficulties:
•Reading difficulty - Reading may be slower or require more effort,especially with unfamiliar words.
•Spelling difficulty - Remembering the correct spelling of words can be challenging.
•Writing difficulty - Organizing ideas, writing quickly or keeping letters and words in the correct order may be difficult.
•Phonological processing - Connecting letters with their corresponding sounds can be challenging.
•Word recognition - Recognising familiar words quickly and automatically may take longer. 
•Memory difficulties -  Remembering sequences , instructions or recently learned information can sometimes be harder.
•Following written instructions -  Long or complex written instructions may be difficult to process. 
•Note-taking - Writing notes while listening to a teacher or lecture can be challenging.
•Visual attention - Focusing on specific letters or words in crowded text may sometimes be challenging. 
•Reading aloud - Reading aloud may be slower and less fluent. 
 Dyslexia games: 
•Letter sound Matching - Match a letter with its correct sound.
•Rhyming Game-  Find words that rhyme ,such as cat-bat. 
•Word scramble - Arrange mixed-up letters to make a word.
•Letter swapping Game- change one letter at a time, such as cat  => bat=> bag etc.
•Word picture Matching - Match a written word with the correct picture.
•Find the word  - Find Target word among similar -looking words.
•Memory Card Game - Match pairs of letters,words,or pictures.
